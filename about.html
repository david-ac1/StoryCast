About the archive

Universal media for every citizen

A recording nobody can access is a recording lost. Every transmission we publish includes captions, a verbatim transcript, and keyboard-friendly controls.

What every transmission includes

Synchronized captions

Video stories include WebVTT captions with speaker names and meaningful sound descriptions.

Verbatim transcripts

Full text sits beside each recording, timestamped and searchable for quick reference.

Keyboard access

Landmarks, visible focus, large targets, and semantic controls support keyboard and screen-reader users.
